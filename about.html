A Journey Born of Passion

Founded in 2019 on the pristine shores of Agatti Island, FAROWIN Tours & Travels began with a simple dream — to share the breathtaking beauty of Lakshadweep with the world, and to take our travelers beyond, to the finest destinations across the globe.

What started as a small island operation has grown into a trusted name in travel, serving over 50,000 happy travelers and counting. Yet, despite our growth, we've never lost sight of what matters most — personalized service, authentic experiences, and the genuine warmth of island hospitality.

Today, FAROWIN stands as Lakshadweep's premier travel partner, offering everything from local island getaways to international luxury escapes, all crafted with the same care and attention to detail that defined us from day one.

Travel isn't just about reaching a destination — it's about the memories you carry home. At FAROWIN, we craft journeys worth remembering.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit to visit Lakshadweep? +

Yes, all visitors (including Indian citizens) need an entry permit to visit Lakshadweep. Don't worry — we handle the entire permit process for you as part of our package. Just provide the required documents and we'll take care of the rest.

What's the best time to visit Lakshadweep? +

The ideal time to visit Lakshadweep is between October and May, when the weather is pleasant and the seas are calm. December to February is the peak season with the best conditions for water activities like snorkeling and diving.

How do I reach Lakshadweep? +

You can reach Lakshadweep by flight (Agatti Airport) from Kochi, or by ship from Kochi to various islands. We arrange flights, ship bookings, and inter-island transfers as part of our packages — making your journey seamless from start to finish.

What's included in your tour packages? +

Our packages typically include accommodation, meals, transfers, sightseeing, water activities, and permits (where applicable). Each package is detailed clearly with inclusions and exclusions. We also offer customizable packages where you choose what you want included.

Can I customize my tour package? +

Absolutely! We specialize in custom tours tailored to your interests, budget, and travel style. Whether it's a honeymoon, family trip, group tour, or solo adventure — share your preferences and we'll craft the perfect itinerary just for you.

What's your cancellation policy? +

Cancellation policies vary depending on the package, travel dates, and proximity to your trip. Generally, cancellations made 30+ days before travel get higher refunds, while last-minute cancellations may have limited refunds. Full details are shared at the time of booking.

Do you offer international tours? +

Yes! Beyond Lakshadweep, we offer curated international packages to Maldives, Dubai, Southeast Asia, Europe, and many more destinations. Our team handles flights, visas, accommodation, and complete itinerary planning.

How do I make a payment? +

We accept payments via UPI, bank transfer, debit/credit cards, and major digital wallets. Typically a booking advance is required to confirm the trip, with the balance paid before departure. Our team will guide you through the process when you book.

Is travel insurance included? +

Travel insurance is not included by default but is highly recommended, especially for international tours. We can help arrange comprehensive travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and lost baggage at competitive rates.

How can I contact you for support during my trip? +

Our team is available 24/7 throughout your trip. You can reach us anytime via phone (+91 94957 68129) or WhatsApp. We'll be there to assist with any questions, changes, or emergencies that come up during your travels.
